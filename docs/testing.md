## Unit tests

The tests are in `tests/`, one doctest file per area (`tabs_test.cpp`, `side_navigation_test.cpp` and so on), built into `build/Release/lamina_tests.exe`. `ctest --preset release` runs it with three tests that check the resource build errors ([Resources](resources.md#tests)) , `mcu_tests`, the GoogleTest tests of the vendored material-color-utilities, and `gallery_layout_412`, `gallery_layout_800` and `gallery_layout_1440`, the layout check of the gallery ([below](#layout-check)). `lamina_tests.exe -tc="*scroll*"` runs only the matching test cases.

`tests/support.hpp` gives the shared fixtures: `fonts()`, `icon_font()` and `scheme()`, `settle(widget)` (steps 240 frames of 1/60 s), `click(widget, x, y)` and `count_color`. Widgets are tested without a window: build one, give it a width and events, step it and read its rectangles or paint it on a `thorvg::Canvas` and read pixels.

The test fonts and their license are listed under [Attribution](../README.md#attribution).

## Offscreen capture

The gallery is `examples/components/gallery.hpp`, behind a small host interface, so `build/Release/components_capture.exe` drives it without a window: it renders frames with ThorVG from scripted pointer, key, text and wheel events at a fixed 60 frames per second, finds its targets by accessible name, draws the pointer and the last key itself, and writes the pixels as raw BGRA (`size <scale>`; `still <scene> <light|dark> <scale> <file>` for one frame, with scenes for every page (`chips`, `chips_focus`, `chips_hover`, `chips_disabled`, `lists`, `lists_focus`, `lists_disabled`, `bars`, `bars_scrolled`); `video <scene> <light|dark> <scale>` to standard output, for ffmpeg, with `filter_chips`, `input_chip`, `card`, `list_keys`, `badge` and `app_bar` for the components of the second stage and `segments`, `range_slider`, `date_picker`, `date_range` and `time_picker` for the third; the stills of the third stage are `inputs`, `inputs_focus`, `inputs_disabled`, `dates`, `dates_years`, `dates_input`, `dates_dialog`, `dates_range_dialog`, `dates_docked`, `dates_japanese`, `dates_focus`, `dates_disabled`, `times`, `times_24h`, `times_dialog`, `times_input_dialog`, `times_focus`, `times_disabled`, and `inputs_tooltip`, `dates_tooltip` and `times_tooltip` for the name tooltips on the icon-only segments and the pickers' icon buttons; the stills of the fourth stage are `sheets`, `sheets_modal`, `sheets_expanded`, `sheets_side_modal`, `sheets_side_detached`, `sheets_side_standard`, `sheets_focus`, `sheets_tooltip`, `sheets_disabled`, `drawers`, `drawers_modal`, `drawers_dismissible`, `drawers_permanent`, `drawers_rail`, `drawers_hiding`, `drawers_focus`, `drawers_disabled`, `search`, `search_full`, `search_docked`, `search_focus`, `search_tooltip` and `search_disabled`, and its videos `bottom_sheet`, `side_sheet`, `drawer` and `search`, whose drags and flings carry timestamps so that their releases have a velocity; the fifth stage's stills are `groups`, `groups_pressed`, `groups_overflow`, `groups_split`, `groups_focus`, `groups_tooltip`, `groups_collapsed`, `groups_disabled`, `progress`, `progress_running`, `progress_handoff`, `toolbars`, `toolbars_scrolled`, `toolbars_menu`, `toolbars_focus`, `toolbars_tooltip` and `toolbars_disabled`, and its videos `button_groups`, `split_button`, `fab_menu`, `loading`, `wavy_progress` and `toolbars`; the sixth stage's stills are `adaptive`, `adaptive_compact`, `adaptive_medium`, `adaptive_expanded`, `adaptive_large`, `adaptive_detail`, `adaptive_supporting`, `adaptive_handle`, `adaptive_focus`, `adaptive_disabled`, `desktop`, `desktop_focus`, `desktop_editor`, `desktop_editor_scrolled`, `desktop_divider`, `desktop_editing` and `desktop_disabled`, and its videos `adaptive_sweep`, `adaptive_panes`, `pane_divider`, `numeric_scrub`, `timeline_scroll` and `density`, and the capture draws the resize and text cursors the gallery asks for; the seventh stage's stills are `files`, `files_file_menu`, `files_edit_menu`, `files_context_edge`, `files_sorted`, `files_resized`, `files_scrolled`, `files_dense` and `files_disabled`, and its videos `files_menubar`, `files_context`, `files_table` and `files_scroll`, which right click, Ctrl- and Shift-click and press chords such as Alt+F and Ctrl+S; `tree` prints the accessibility tree after a scene). It is a development tool that needs no display; CI builds it and runs only its layout check.

`--window <width>x<height>` (in dp) sets the window size of every mode; the default is 1440 × 860 dp. A page scene first scrolls its page tab into view with the wheel; any other target that has scrolled out of view stops the scene with "… is out of view" instead of clicking empty space. The stills `scrolled` (wheels the Basics page down) and `focus_reveal` (tabs to the Name field, which scrolls it into view) show the page scrolling. The scenes `gallery_pages` (clicks through seven pages), `gallery_pages_keys` (walks the page tabs with the arrow keys) and `gallery_resize` (narrows the window to just below each window size class breakpoint and to 360 dp, then widens it again; the space outside the window is painted grey) are for recording the gallery itself. `times_resize` narrows the Times page from the window's width to 360 dp and widens it again; the stills `times_side_scrolled` (shift and the wheel over the horizontal time picker) and `times_side_focus` (Tab to its dial) show its sideways scrolling.

Example: one frame of the Basics page at a phone width, and a video for ffmpeg:

```
components_capture --window 412x860 still default light 1 basics.bgra
components_capture video gallery_resize dark 1 | ffmpeg -f rawvideo -pix_fmt bgra -s 1440x860 -r 60 -i - out.mp4
```

`components_capture size <scale>` prints the pixel size of a frame for the same options.

### Layout check

`components_capture [--window <width>x<height>] check` opens each of the fifteen pages in turn, lets it settle and reports every widget that passes the window's right edge, has been squeezed to nothing in one direction, or overlaps a widget of another block. A block is a section, caption or item the page places (`Gallery::layout_blocks`); widgets inside one block, such as the parts of a frame, are not compared with each other. It exits with 1 when it finds a problem. Components that cannot fit a window can be listed in `CaptureStyle::known_overflows`; they are printed as known and not counted while they start at the content's left edge, and the check fails if one is no longer on its page. The list is empty: at 412 dp the segmented rows and split buttons share the width there is, and the horizontal time picker scrolls sideways inside its card. After the pages, the check also taps `CaptureStyle::redraw_target` (Send on Basics) with a touch whose down, up and leave arrive in one frame, runs frames while the gallery animates, and then compares the idle frame with one more frame without input; it reports a problem when they differ by more than `CaptureStyle::redraw_tolerance` levels, since the frame loop would then show the idle frame until the next event ([App structure](app-structure.md)). It checks Back three times on a fresh gallery: with nothing open the first Back is handed to the host (`GalleryHost::system_back`); after Tab and Enter on `CaptureStyle::back_menu` the first Back closes the menu and only the second is handed on; after Tab to `CaptureStyle::back_field` the first Back takes the focus from the field and only the second is handed on. On every page of the check, with nothing open, the first Back is handed on. After Tab and Enter on `CaptureStyle::back_dialog` and after the `CaptureStyle::back_sheet_scene` scene (the modal bottom sheet), the first Back is not handed on and a later one is, within `CaptureStyle::back_press_limit` presses. Escape without `KeyEvent::back` is never handed on. It also checks that a gallery tells its host whether it is dark, once at start and again when the theme is switched (`GalleryHost::set_dark_theme`, which the window passes to the system bars, [Android](android.md#system-bars)). CTest runs it at 412, 800 and 1440 dp.

## Touch injection on Windows

`tools/touch-inject/` drives a real lamina window with synthetic touch on Windows, so the SDL touch path, the client-drawn title bar and the touch keyboard can be checked on a machine without a touch screen. It is Python with ctypes and Pillow; run it with `python -B` so that no `__pycache__` is written. `inject.py` holds the logic (finding the window, the guard, `InjectTouchInput`, window capture) and `cli.py` only parses arguments.

```
build/Release/components.exe --title-bar
python -B tools/touch-inject/cli.py --pid <pid> info
python -B tools/touch-inject/cli.py --pid <pid> run tools/touch-inject/scenarios/basics-send.toml --out shots --log shots/log.jsonl
python -B tools/touch-inject/cli.py --pid <pid> run --out shots --step 'action="tap", x=170, y=562' --step 'action="capture", name="switch"'
python -B tools/touch-inject/cli.py --pid <pid> capture shot.png
python -B tools/touch-inject/cli.py --pid <pid> restore
```

The window is the one visible top-level window of the process `--pid`. A scenario is a TOML file of `[[step]]` tables, and `--step` adds one inline table each; steps from both run in order. Coordinates are relative to the client area, in dp by default (`unit = "px"` for pixels), converted with `GetDpiForWindow`. A gesture's path is resolved to screen pixels when it starts, so dragging the title bar moves the window under a finger that keeps its screen path.

| Action | Fields |
|---|---|
| `tap` | `x`, `y`, `hold` (default `timing.tap_hold_seconds`) |
| `press` | as `tap`, `hold` defaulting to `timing.long_press_seconds` |
| `drag` | `from`, `to` (`[x, y]`, or a list of them for several fingers), `duration`, `profile` (`linear`, `ease_out`, `ease_in`), `hold_start`, `hold_end`; with `hold_end = 0` the finger lifts while moving, which flings |
| `capture` | `name`, `settle` (seconds to wait first) |
| `wait` | `seconds` |
| `activate` | brings the window to the foreground and out of the minimized state: `ShowWindow(SW_RESTORE)` if it is minimized, then `SetForegroundWindow`, then minimize and restore if Windows refuses it; waits up to `timing.foreground_wait_seconds`, checking `timing.foreground_polls` times |
| `resize` | `width`, `height` of the client area |
| `restore` | `ShowWindow(SW_RESTORE)`, for after a minimize |
| `watch` | logs the top-level windows of `watch.keyboard_processes` with their DWM cloaked state |
| `info` | logs the DPI scale and the client rectangle |

`tap`, `press` and `drag` take `gap` (the wait after the gesture) and `snapshot`, a list of `{at, name}` captures taken while the finger is down, `at` seconds after it touched. Frames are `injection.frame_interval_seconds` apart; the wait between them sleeps `timing.sleep_fraction` of the time left until less than `timing.spin_threshold_seconds` is left, then spins. Every default lives in `tools/touch-inject/defaults.toml` (`--settings` reads another file).

Before every touch frame the guard checks that the process still has that one window, that it is the foreground window and not minimized, that the point is inside its client area and on a monitor, and that `WindowFromPoint` at the point belongs to it. If a check fails, the fingers that are down are cancelled, the run stops and `run` exits with 3. Captures use `PrintWindow` with `PW_CLIENTONLY | PW_RENDERFULLCONTENT`, so they hold the window's client area only and never other windows.

Findings on Windows 11 without a touch screen (SDL 3.4.10):

- `InitializeTouchInjection` and `InjectTouchInput` work without a digitizer (`SM_DIGITIZER` 0). SDL receives the contacts as `WM_TOUCH`, sends finger events and the mouse events it makes from them with `SDL_TOUCH_MOUSEID`, and drops the mouse messages Windows promotes from touch (`GetMouseMessageSource`). A tap toggles a switch once.
- Hover after a touch. Observed: after a tap, the cursor (`GetCursorPos`) is at the tap point, and once the window is restored from minimized or resized, the control under that point shows mouse hover until the next touch; once the hover also appeared on the control under the last touch with no window change made by the tool. The source shows one path: when the window gains focus, SDL sends a mouse motion with `SDL_GLOBAL_MOUSE_ID` at the message position (`WIN_UpdateFocus`), so the restore case comes from SDL. For the resize case and the case without a known trigger, it is inferred, not checked, that Windows sends a mouse move without the touch marker because the window changed under the cursor.
- A window restored from minimized laid out from a tiny size; fixed. Observed before the fix: with `--title-bar`, the client rectangle of the minimized gallery is 160 × 28 px; after a restore the content sprang back from a collapsed layout and the status read the width class again, and a tap sent right after the restore missed its control (3 of 3). With the system frame the minimized client rectangle is 0 × 0 and the content did not move. The source shows that SDL ignores the size of a minimized window in its events (`WM_WINDOWPOSCHANGED`) but `SDL_GetWindowSizeInPixels` returns `GetClientRect` whenever its width is above 0, and falls back to the last size otherwise; `Window::measure` read that size every frame. Now `Window::measure` keeps the last frame size while SDL flags the window `SDL_WINDOW_MINIMIZED` (`layout::frame_pixel_size`, on every platform): the first frames after a restore show the full layout, and a tap right after the restore hits (3 of 3).
- No touch keyboard. Observed: tapping a text field focuses it, and the window of `TextInputHost.exe` stays cloaked. The source shows that SDL 3.4.10 has `ShowScreenKeyboard` only in its GDK build, not on desktop Windows. From general knowledge, not checked here: Windows also brings up the touch keyboard on its own for a focused editable element it sees through UI Automation, which lamina-ui does not expose, and by default only when no hardware keyboard is attached; this machine has one, and its settings were not changed, so which of these keeps the keyboard away was not separated.
