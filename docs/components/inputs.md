## Text field

`lamina/widgets/text_field.hpp`: the outlined text field. Like the other widgets it takes `TextFieldOptions` (the label, `multiline` and the clipboard) and the fonts. It is 280 dp wide, or wider with `set_width`, and has `width()` and `height()` like the other widgets. `handle(PointerEvent)` returns true when a press lands inside the field, which then takes the focus. A touch focuses the field on the tap instead, when the finger lifts inside it without the page having taken the drag, so a scroll that starts on the field neither focuses it nor takes the focus from another field; a touch drag does not select. The label floats onto the outline with a spring when the field is focused or filled, and the outline path leaves a gap behind it (a field without a label keeps its outline whole), so the field paints nothing but itself and needs no background color; the caret blinks without redrawing in between (`Window::wake_after`); Japanese input goes through the system IME: the composition is drawn underlined in place and the candidate window is placed at the caret (`Window::set_text_input`). Shift with the arrow keys, Home or End extends a selection; dragging selects a range, a double click selects a word (in Japanese, a run of kanji, hiragana or katakana), and a triple click or Ctrl+A selects everything. Typing, an IME commit, Backspace and Delete replace the selection. Ctrl+Left/Right move by word and Ctrl+Backspace/Delete delete by word. Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V use the clipboard given in `TextFieldOptions` (`Window::clipboard()` for the system clipboard, any `input::Clipboard` in tests). A single-line field scrolls sideways to keep the caret in view and clips its text to the field (`Canvas::push_clip`); Enter submits. With `TextFieldOptions::multiline` the text wraps to the field's width and the field grows by one line at a time from 56 dp (16 dp above and below the text). Enter inserts a newline and Ctrl+Enter submits; Up and Down move between lines keeping the caret's horizontal position; Home and End go to the start and end of the line, and with Ctrl to the start and end of the text. `TextFieldOptions::placeholder` is shown where the input goes, in on-surface-variant Body L, while the field is empty: with a label only while the field has the focus, without a label always. It fades in on the slow effects spring when an empty field takes the focus or its text is cleared, and out on the fast effects spring; the first character, or an IME composition, removes it at once, as Compose's text field does. `TextFieldOptions::supporting_text` (or `set_supporting_text`) is shown 4 dp below the field in Body S, and `set_error(true)` turns it, the outline, the label and the caret to the error color (the hovered outline to on-error-container) on the default effects spring, and marks the field `invalid` for accessibility. `TextFieldOptions::filter` maps or drops each typed or pasted character (`widgets::ascii_digit` keeps digits, full-width ones included), `max_length` caps the text, and `mask` (such as `MM/dd/yyyy`: letters are slots, other characters are delimiters) keeps only the slot characters in the text while showing each delimiter once the part before it is complete, as Compose's date input does; the caret and the selection skip the delimiters. `min_width` lowers the 280 dp minimum. `TextFieldOptions::enabled` and `set_enabled` disable the field: it takes no focus, pointer or text, and its outline (on-surface at 12 %), text, label and placeholder (on-surface at 38 %) fade to the disabled colors. With `TextFieldOptions::accepts_tab`, Tab types a tab character at the caret, replacing the selection, instead of moving the focus, while Shift+Tab and any Tab with Ctrl are left to the focus order (`FocusManager` moves the focus out on Ctrl+Tab, below); such a field also keeps the tabs in pasted text and in `set_text`, where any other field turns them into spaces. While the field has the focus, Ctrl+Z undoes its own edits and Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes them. Consecutive typing is one step. A step ends at whitespace typed after a word, when the caret or the selection moves without an edit, when the field gains or loses the focus, when typing turns into deleting or back, and after a pause of more than 1 s (`undo_pause_seconds`). A paste, a cut, a typed tab, an IME commit and an edit that replaces the selection are steps of their own. Undo and redo bring back the caret and the selection the text had. A new edit after an undo drops what could be redone, the history keeps 100 steps (`undo_depth`), and `set_text` clears it. When the field has nothing to undo or redo, the key does nothing in the field and `handles_history_key(e)` is false, so the program can give the key to an undo of its own, as the to-do list does; `can_undo()` and `can_redo()` say the same. While an IME composition is open, the field takes the undo keys (`handles_history_key` is true) and does nothing with them, so the composition stays as it is and the program's undo does not run either. The pause is measured with the time given to `step`, which keeps coming while the field has the focus because the caret blink wakes the window. `build/Release/text_field.exe` is a small form whose message field is multi-line and accepts tabs: Tab types a tab there, and Ctrl+Tab or Shift+Tab goes back to the name field. `TextFieldOptions::outlined = false` draws the field without its outline and label, and `padding_start` and `padding_end` (also `set_padding`) leave room for icons drawn by its owner, as the search bar does.

Japanese input with an IME is described in [Text and fonts](../text-and-fonts.md#japanese-input).

## Numeric field

`lamina/widgets/numeric_field.hpp`: `NumericField`, a compact numeric field (40 dp at density 0, 28 dp at least) with its label at the start or inside and a unit at the end. Dragging across it scrubs the value by steps, a click edits it, Up and Down step it (Shift, Page Up and Page Down by the large step), Enter commits and Escape restores. Values stay in the range, and text that is not a number marks the field invalid.

## Search bar

`lamina/widgets/search_bar.hpp`: Compose's `SearchBar` with `ExpandedFullScreenSearchBar` and `ExpandedDockedSearchBar`. The bar is a 56 dp pill on surface-container-high, 360 to 720 dp wide, with a search icon, a query field and an optional trailing icon button. A click, Down or typing expands it on the slow spatial spring into a view of results (a `ListView`; `set_results` replaces them as the query changes): full-screen below 600 dp of area width, where the surface grows from the bar to the area and its corners go square, and docked from 600 dp, where the results drop down below the bar, at least 240 dp and at most 2/3 of the area tall. Expanded, the leading icon is a back button, Down moves into the results and Up from the first comes back, Enter in the field reports `searched`, Enter or a click on a result reports `chosen`, Escape or the back button collapses it on the default spatial spring, and Tab stays inside. The field is named "Search" and says "Suggestions below" while expanded. Pushed on an `OverlayStack`, which clears the page's focus, the expanded bar puts the focus back on its field on its next `step`; a modal rail opened from the keyboard does the same for the rail.
